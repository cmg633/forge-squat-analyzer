## PART 1: Customer Pitch (5 minutes)

### Opening Hook (30 seconds)
*"Most people squat wrong. Not because they don't care — but because they can't see themselves. A mirror shows you a front view. A coach costs \$100/hour. And both tell you what's wrong in real time, not on video. Forge changes that."*

### The Problem (60 seconds)
- The barbell squat is the most important strength exercise. It's also the most commonly performed incorrectly.
- Poor depth, excessive forward lean, bar drift — these aren't just performance issues. They're injury risks.
- Existing tools: fitness trackers count reps, YouTube coaches generalize, expensive coaching is inaccessible.
- **What's missing:** An objective, evidence-based assessment you can run on your own footage, any time.

### The Solution (90 seconds)
*"Record a 20-second video from the side. Upload it. In under 2 minutes, Forge tells you:"*

1. How many reps you completed
2. Whether each rep achieved proper depth (hip crease below patella — the actual standard from Starting Strength)
3. Your back angle, bar path, hip drive, and gaze direction
4. What to fix — with the exact page of the reference that defines the standard
5. What we **couldn't** assess, and why — because honesty matters

*"No black boxes. Every finding shows its source. Every estimate is labeled as an estimate."*

### Differentiation (60 seconds)
| Forge | Competitors |
|-------|-------------|
| Evidence-based criteria from reference literature | Generalized fitness advice |
| "Cannot assess" when uncertain | Confident wrong answers |
| Free, runs on a \$0/month stack | \$30-100/month subscriptions |
| PWA — installable, works on any device | Native app only |
| Hot-reloadable skill YAML | Hard-coded rules |

### Call to Action (30 seconds)
*"Try it today. Upload any side-view squat video. No account required. Free forever on the basic tier. Let's see your lift."*

### Step 2: Upload a Good Video (2 minutes)
- Drag and drop a side-view squat video (use the common sample)
- **Show real upload progress** — "Uploading: 67%"
- Watch the stage progress update in real time:
  - Validating → Tracking pose → Tracking bar → Counting reps → Assessing → Rendering → Done
- Point out: "These are real API calls — no loading fake for show."

### Step 3: Results — Bento Grid (3 minutes)
- **Hero tile:** Annotated video with red skeleton overlay + cyan bar path trail
  - Scrub through — rep markers on the timeline
  - Point out: rep badge appears in the corner during each rep
- **Summary tile:** Overall verdict, top 3 fixes
- **Quality gate tile:** ✅ Side view detected, ✅ Full body visible, ✅ Camera steady
- **Findings tiles:**
  - Green = meets standard (Depth ✅, Hip Drive ✅)
  - Red = does not meet (Back Angle ❌)
  - Grey = cannot assess (Stance Width ⬜ — requires front view)
- Click a finding → seeking to the exact frame, bottom sheet shows full detail
- Point out: "Source page 13, Fig 2-13. Verbatim quote from the reference. This is not a made-up number."
- Point out "IMPLEMENTATION ASSUMPTION" badge on back angle: "The PDF says 'about 45°'. We document that our ±10° range is our engineering choice, not the book's."
- **Bar path chart:** Recharts line chart showing bar x-position over the rep. Should be flat = vertical path.
- **Coaching tile:** Generated text. If Gemini key set, says "Generated by Gemini." Otherwise: "Template coaching — set GEMINI_API_KEY for personalized text."

## Likely Objections & Answers

### "MediaPipe landmarks aren't accurate enough."
*"You're right that landmarks have uncertainty — especially in monocular 2D. That's exactly why every measurement is labeled 'ESTIMATE' with a confidence score, and why we show an 'Observed vs Estimated' legend. We never claim precision we don't have."*

### "The depth assessment is just comparing y-coordinates."
*"Yes. We document that explicitly as an ESTIMATE in the UI and in the code. The alternative — 3D reconstruction — would require stereo cameras or running MiDaS depth estimation, adding latency and GPU cost. The y-coordinate proxy works for typical side-view recordings. We flag borderline cases as 'borderline parallel' rather than making a confident call."*

### "What if the bar goes out of frame or is occluded?"
*"The CSRT tracker marks frames as 'tracker lost' with decreasing confidence. Any metric that depends on bar position reports 'cannot assess' if the tracker is lost for more than a few frames. We show this in the quality gate tile."*

### "Why not use a fine-tuned model for squat assessment?"
*"We deliberately chose a rule-based skill engine backed by a YAML file. This makes the assessment auditable and contestable — you can read the YAML and see exactly what rule produced each verdict. A fine-tuned model would be a black box. The LLM is only used for text generation, not for decisions."*

### "Where's the iOS app?"
*"iOS App Store requires a \$99/year developer account and Apple review. The PWA works on iOS Safari — installable via 'Add to Home Screen', full-screen, offline capable for the app shell. Native iOS is on the roadmap."*

### "What happens to my video?"
*"Videos are stored ephemerally in memory on the backend. They're automatically deleted after 6 hours. No video is ever associated with a user account — we don't have user accounts. History is stored locally on your device via IndexedDB."*
